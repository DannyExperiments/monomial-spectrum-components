# Research status

For all sufficiently large integers q, the dimension spectrum for continued fractions with digits in {n^q : n ≥ 1} has at least 2^(floor(log₂ q)−2) connected components, and hence at least q/8.

Finite replay checks the symbolic and numerical examples used to illustrate the analytic proof. The manuscript contains the proof. No Lean formalization was attempted. External peer review is pending.
