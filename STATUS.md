# Research status

For all sufficiently large integers q, the dimension spectrum for continued fractions with digits in {n^q : n ≥ 1} has at least 2^(floor(log₂ q)−2) connected components, and hence at least q/8.

Finite replay checks the symbolic and numerical examples used to illustrate the analytic proof. The manuscript contains the proof.  External peer review is pending.

For any real spectrum, a realized increasing list of 2^k representatives with a missing spectrum point in each adjacent gap gives an injection of those representatives into its genuine connected components, including for a nonclosed spectrum. The eigenfunction and pressure estimates establishing these gaps for the monomial digits, and the choice of k as a function of q, are in the manuscript. [Lean coverage](formal/closing/README.md)
