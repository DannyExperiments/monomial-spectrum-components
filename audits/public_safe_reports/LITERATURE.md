# Literature comparison

Chitanga, Lemmens and Nussbaum ask in §9 whether the number of component intervals for the monomial alphabet is bounded uniformly in q. Chitanga, Lemmens and Nussbaum prove the number of components is finite for each exponent and compute some small-exponent counts. Their §4 gives an exact singleton calculation and §8 uses pressure-tail comparisons for monomial alphabets; Chitanga’s thesis gives additional small-exponent counts and leaves boundedness unresolved. The contribution here is the uniform growing prefix and the resulting exponential count in its length.

The primary-source and theorem-architecture search through November 27, 2026 found no exact collision for the claimed scope. Confidence is moderate. Related work on Fibonacci digit alphabets by Dawar and Priyadarshi treats a different family. The companion catalog problem Q1536 uses a fixed geometric alphabet and measures above-threshold slices.
