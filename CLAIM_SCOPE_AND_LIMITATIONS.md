# Claim scope

For all sufficiently large integers q, the dimension spectrum for continued fractions with digits in {n^q : n ≥ 1} has at least 2^(floor(log₂ q)−2) connected components, and hence at least q/8.

The theorem is asymptotic. It gives a lower bound and leaves the exact counts and the location of count jumps open. Finite replay checks the symbolic and numerical examples used to illustrate the analytic proof. The manuscript contains the proof. No Lean formalization was attempted.
