# Worked example

For large q with floor(log₂ q)≥5, the adjacent patterns 011 and 100 first differ at m=3. The former can include every digit larger than 3^q; the latter has a smallest realization {1, 2^q, 3^q}. The tail estimate gives a strict gap between these extremal dimensions. Eight patterns already occur on these three digits, and the proof extends to floor(log₂ q)−2 digits.
